## Short summary (one line)

Sell your loot by zone and quality without losing anything valuable, and see every character's keystone, Great Vault and lockouts at a glance.

## Description

**eSaith Bag Filter** remembers where every item you loot came from. Visit a vendor, pick the dungeon or zone, and sell everything from it in one click. Anything you want to keep is protected.

### Sell safely
- Pick a zone (or *All zones*), filter by quality, or search by name.
- **Click** an item to keep it, **right-click** to hide it for good.
- Keep rules protect BoE items, gear at your item level, uncollected transmog, trade goods, and items worth more on the Auction House (with TradeSkillMaster or Auctionator).
- Big sales ask first, and **Undo Last Sale** buys everything back from the vendor.
- Optional auto-sell junk and auto-repair (guild funds first).
- A small coin marks sellable items in your bags (Blizzard bags and Baganator).

### Track your week
- Every character's **Mythic+ keystone** and **Great Vault** progress, with a reminder when rewards are waiting.
- **Raid and dungeon lockouts** with time until reset.
- **Currencies** each character tracks on their backpack.
- **Recent runs**: what each dungeon, raid or delve run earned.

### Little helpers
Quick loot, auto-greed greens, quest helper (hold Shift to skip), container opener, coordinates under the minimap, minimap button, per-character settings and a resizable, scalable window.

Open it from the **eFilter** tab on any vendor, the minimap button, the addon compartment, or `/efilter`.
